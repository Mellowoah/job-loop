How to play

  • Throw downSwipe up, tap, or press Throw. A soft toss is about 5 spins. A hard snap is up to 18. The dial counts the spins you have left.
  • Swing for tricksWhile it sleeps, drag to move your hand. Swing wide for Whip and Around the World, rock 3× for Rock the Baby, drag up to reach it to the floor and Walk the Dog.
  • Bind it homePull down, tap, or press Bind while spins are left. Tricks in one throw stack ×1, ×1.5, ×2… If the counter hits zero, the whole score clears.
  • Tilt mode (phones)Tilt to move your hand. Flick the phone forward to throw and pull it back toward you to bind. Touch always works too.
  • Carnival platesPlates sit on the floor. Level 1 is two large plates that hold still. Each level they shrink toward the size of the yo-yo and move on one path: sway, drift, patrol, then a loop. Higher levels also take more hits. A gold plate pays half again. Smash another within 3 seconds for a chain, up to ×3.
  • Grades, chests, bossesA centered hit reads Perfect, Great, or Good. Plate points still follow the chain, up to ×3. Trick combos flash ×2, ×3, and ×5 when the score math already lands there. Every 5th level opens a cosmetic chest and, in Carnival, a boss plate that takes a few hits. Looks never change the throw. A missed day or week does not take anything away.
